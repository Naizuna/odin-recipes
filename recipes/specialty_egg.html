Specialty Egg Recipe


Home

Description

This is the specialty egg recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • Mayonaise
  • Ground Black Pepper
  • Magic Sarap

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add the magic sarap, mayonaise, and black pepper into the egg.
  3. Mix the ingredients together until the mayonaise dissolves.
  4. Pour the mixture into a pan with hot oil.
  5. Fry the egg until the bottom is cooked while the top is still raw.
  6. Place the egg on a plate and serve.
