Lasagna Recipe


Home

Description

This is the lasagna recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Beef
  • 1 lb Lasagna Noodles
  • 1 can Tomato Sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until no longer pink.
  4. Add the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer the cooked noodles, meat sauce, and any additional ingredients in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the top is golden brown.
